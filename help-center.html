Frequently Asked Questions

ResolveOne uses a multi-step AI diagnostic engine. It first analyzes the ticket description through natural language processing to identify the core issue. It then cross-references the Knowledge Base for similar resolved cases, checks historical resolution patterns, and calculates a confidence score. If the confidence is above 85%, the AI automatically suggests the resolution and creates a detailed explanation.

Tickets are assigned based on category matching, team workload balancing, and skill-based routing. The AI evaluates the issue category detected in the ticket, checks current team capacity across departments, and routes the ticket to the most qualified available team. Escalation rules apply automatically for priority tickets.

Yes, closed tickets can be reopened within 14 days of resolution. Navigate to My Tickets, find the closed ticket, and select the reopen option. The ticket will be reassigned to the original support team with full context preserved. After 14 days, we recommend creating a new ticket and referencing the previous one for continuity.

Every ticket created in ResolveOne is assigned a unique ID visible in My Tickets. The dashboard shows real-time status updates including assignment, diagnosis, resolution progress, and SLA compliance. You can also view the full timeline of events for each ticket including AI actions, human interventions, and escalations.

The Knowledge Base uses semantic search powered by natural language understanding. It indexes article content, titles, categories, and metadata. When you search, it finds the most relevant articles based on meaning rather than exact keyword matching. Results are ranked by relevance score and AI recommendation confidence.

Yes, the platform supports manual ticket creation and management without AI assistance. However, the AI features significantly improve resolution speed (up to 4× faster in testing) and reduce manual workload. Disabling AI means you lose automated diagnosis, smart recommendations, and proactive routing.
